## Convert DAI from Ethereum to xDAI

### 1. Buy DAI

DAI is a widely known stablecoin on the Ethereum blockchain.

Dai can be purchased on many popular exchanges, which many are listed on [https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/multi-collateral-dai/markets/](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/multi-collateral-dai/markets/). It is also available on many decentralized exchanges like [Oasis](https://oasis.app/trade/), [KyberSwap](https://kyberswap.com/), and [Uniswap](https://uniswap.exchange/).

After purchasing and sending DAI to your wallet, you can use [Gnosis Bridge](https://bridge.gnosischain.com/) to convert from DAI to xDAI.

### 2. Convert DAI to xDAI

First, go to [Gnosis Bridge](https://bridge.gnosischain.com/) and connect your wallet to the Ethereum Mainnet.

<Frame><img src="/images/getting-started/deposit-tokens/on-gnosis/deposit-xdai/01-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Select DAI as the origin token. Make sure that the **Transfer from** section is set to Ethereum. If not, you can click on the swap icon in the center. 

Once you've selected DAI, the UI will automatically select the destination token (xDAI) for you.

<Frame><img src="/images/getting-started/deposit-tokens/on-gnosis/deposit-xdai/02-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Input the desired amount and click **Bridge**.

You can check the transaction details with the [bridge explorer](https://bridge.gnosischain.com/bridge-explorer).

<Frame><img src="/images/getting-started/deposit-tokens/on-gnosis/deposit-xdai/03-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Once you've entered a transaction hash or your wallet address, a list of transactions will appear.
