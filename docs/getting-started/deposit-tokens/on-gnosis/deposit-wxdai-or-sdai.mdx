title: "Deposit wxDAI or sDAI"
---
Besides xDAI, users with wXDAI or sDAI tokens in their wallets can also interact with Seer.

Most methods of obtaining wxDAI or sDAI require users to have xDAI first.

## Wrap xDAI

Wrap your xDai in [Wrap Eth](https://wrapeth.com/) connected with [MetaMask to Gnosis](https://docs.gnosischain.com/tools/wallets/metamask/).

## Interact with the respective smart contracts

Another way to convert xDAI to wxDAI or sDAI is to interact directly with their respective smart contracts on [Gnosis Block Explorer](https://gnosisscan.io/).

### wxDAI

Block Explorer Url:

<Card title="https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xe91D153E0b41518A2Ce8Dd3D7944Fa863463a97d" href="https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xe91D153E0b41518A2Ce8Dd3D7944Fa863463a97d" />

1. Visit the url, select **Contract** -> **Write Contract**.
2. Click **Connect to Web3** to connect your wallet. Make sure the network is **Gnosis**.
3. Under **deposit**, input the amount of xDAI you want to convert.

<Frame><img src="/images/getting-started/deposit-tokens/on-gnosis/deposit-wxdai-or-sdai/01-android-chrome-192x192.png" alt="" /></Frame>

### sDAI

Block Explorer Url:

<Card title="https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xD499b51fcFc66bd31248ef4b28d656d67E591A94" href="https://gnosisscan.io/address/0xD499b51fcFc66bd31248ef4b28d656d67E591A94" />

1. Visit the url, select **Contract** -> **Write Contract**.
2. Click **Connect to Web3** to connect your wallet. Make sure the network is **Gnosis**.
3. Under **depositXDAI**, input the amount of xDAI you want to convert and the receiver address (you can paste your wallet address here).
