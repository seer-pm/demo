---
title: "Leaderboard and Trader Score"
---

The [leaderboard](https://app.seer.pm/leaderboard) ranks wallets by their trading profit and loss, in USD, across Seer markets. Alongside P/L, volume, ROI and traded markets, each wallet carries a **Trader Score**.

## What the Trader Score is

A 0–100 rating of how a wallet traded the markets in a period, derived from its per-market profit and loss distribution.

It is a summary of *how* the profit was made, not just how much. Two wallets that both finished +$10,000 can score very differently: one that made it steadily across twenty markets while losing little scores well; one that made it all on a single lucky market, after losing heavily elsewhere, does not.

## What each tier means

| Tier | Score | What it says about the wallet |
|---|---|---|
| **Elite** | 85–100 | Strong returns spread across many markets, with small losses. |
| **Great** | 70–84.9 | Profitable and consistent, with one weaker component. |
| **Good** | 55–69.9 | Net positive, but concentrated or with meaningful losses. |
| **Average** | 40–54.9 | Around break-even once losses and concentration are counted. |
| **Weak** | 0–39.9 | Losses outweigh gains, or profit came from a single market. |

A tier is only a label for the number — the score itself is what is ranked and sorted.

## How the score is calculated

The score is the weighted mean of five components. Each is measured, then mapped onto 0–100 between a floor and a ceiling, then weighted:

| Weight | Component | What it measures | Scores 0 | Scores 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25% | **Returns** | Profit ÷ capital deployed (ROI) | −25% | +75% |
| 25% | **Profit factor** | Money made ÷ money lost, across markets | 0.5 | 3.0 |
| 20% | **Hit rate** | Share of markets that ended in profit | 35% | 75% |
| 15% | **Loss burn** | Total losses as a share of capital | 50% | 0% |
| 15% | **Breadth** | Whether profit came from many markets or one | all from one market | spread over 3+ |

Click any score on the leaderboard to see these five components for that wallet, with the raw value of each and the points it contributed.

### Two corrections for small books

A wallet that traded three markets and won all three has not proven it can trade — it has a small sample. Two adjustments stop small books from posting perfect numbers:

- **Hit rate** carries five pseudo-markets at 50%, so three wins out of three reads about 73%, not 100%.
- **Profit factor** carries prior mass on both sides worth 5% of capital, so a wallet that never lost reads a finite ratio instead of infinity.

## Why some wallets show no score

A wallet shows **N/A** instead of a score when there is not enough history to judge it fairly:

- fewer than **3 markets** with more than **$1** of capital at risk, or
- less than **$100** of total capital at risk in the period.

This is deliberately N/A and not a `0`: "too little history to judge" is not the same claim as "judged and scored badly". Hover or tap it to see which of the two conditions the wallet missed.

<Note>
The **Traded Markets** column counts every market a wallet touched, while the score only counts markets where more than $1 was at risk. That is why a wallet can show a large number of traded markets and still be unscored — dusted and airdropped positions do not count toward the score.
</Note>

## What the score does not measure

The score reads a wallet's distribution **across markets**, not over time. It cannot see a position that was deep underwater mid-period and recovered, because it only sees where each market finished. "Loss burn" is a total of realised losses, not a maximum drawdown, and "breadth" measures how concentrated the profit was, not how smooth the equity curve looked.

Scores are computed fresh for whatever period, app and chain filter you have selected. Because the score is a weighted mean of ratios, two scores cannot be meaningfully averaged together.
