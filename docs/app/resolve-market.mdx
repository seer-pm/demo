---
title: "Resolve market"
---
You can either wait until the [current answer](/app/report-answer) is final or [raise a dispute](/app/raise-a-dispute) and wait for a ruling. Either way, after you see the following status, you can click on **Report Answer** to resolve the market.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/resolve-market/01-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

The market is resolved. You can now redeem your winning tokens (YES tokens in this case).
