---
title: "Provide Liquidity"
---
Users can provide liquidity for any outcome of their choice. Liquidity providers (LP) will earn a proportion of the fees generated from trading that outcome.

First, visit any market, select an outcome and [mint outcome tokens](/app/mint-merge-redeem-outcome-tokens).

Where the liquidity goes depends on the network:

- **Base, Optimism and Ethereum**: markets trade on Uniswap V4 pools with Seer's limit-order hook, and you add and manage liquidity **inside the app**. See [Uniswap V4 pools](#uniswap-v4-pools-base-optimism-ethereum).
- **Gnosis**: markets trade on Swapr, and the **Add liquidity** button takes you there. See [Swapr pools](#swapr-pools-gnosis).

## Uniswap V4 pools (Base, Optimism, Ethereum)

Each outcome has one pool against the market's collateral (sUSDS on Base and Optimism, sDAI on Ethereum). Pools charge a 0.01% fee on every trade, and that fee goes to the liquidity providers in range.

<Info>
To provide liquidity you need both tokens of the pair: the outcome token and the collateral. Mint outcome tokens first if you don't have any.
</Info>

Click **Add liquidity** on the outcome. The **Liquidity** window opens with the form and, below it, your existing positions in that pool.

**Adding liquidity**

1. Set the **Min price** and **Max price** of the range you want to cover, as prices between 0 and 1. Your tokens only trade, and only earn fees, while the outcome's price is inside that range.
2. If the pool doesn't exist yet, you also set its **starting price** (for example `0.65`). It has to be inside your range.
3. Enter the amount of either token. The other amount is calculated from the price range. If the current price is outside your range, only one of the two tokens is needed.
4. Approve the tokens if asked, then submit.

With a wallet that supports smart accounts, approvals, pool creation and the deposit go out as a single confirmation. Otherwise you confirm each step in turn.

**Your positions**

Under the form, **Your positions** lists each position you hold in the pool with its price range, the current amounts of each token, and whether it is **In range** or **Out of range**.

- Pick **25%**, **50%**, **75%** or **100%** and click **Remove** to withdraw part of the position. **Remove all** withdraws everything and closes the position.
- **Collect fees** sends you the trading fees the position has earned without touching its liquidity.

Your positions across all markets of a network are also listed in the [Portfolio](/app/navigate-our-site) under the **Liquidity** tab.

Since the pools are regular Uniswap V4 pools, you can also open them on [app.uniswap.org](https://app.uniswap.org) from the pool list in the same window.

## Swapr pools (Gnosis)

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/01-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

On each outcome there will be an **Add liquidity** button. Click on it.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/02-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

For market that does not have liquidity, you will be directed to the Swapr site.

For market that already had liquidity, a popup will appear. Click on **Open** to be directed to Swapr as well.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/03-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

On Swapr, check if the selected pair is correct. Normally, you would want to provide liquidity in sDAI. However, in conditional markets, both tokens will be outcome tokens (one is the conditional outcome token, and the other is the parent outcome token).

<Info>
To provide liquidity for a pair of tokens, you must hold both tokens.
</Info>

After this, click on **Set initial price**.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/04-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Set an initial price for the pair. This number will later be used to determine the corresponding amount of each token in the pair you need to provide. If there is an USD price for each token in the pair, this can be calculated automatically for you.

After this, click on **Select a range**.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/05-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Swapr is based on the concept of concentrated liquidity, which allows users to provide their tokens as liquidity for trading within a specific price range. Liquidity providers earn their fee share only within this active liquidity range. This approach can be useful for concentrating your capital on prices you believe will be most actively traded, enabling you to effectively earn more per unit of capital.

If you're unsure about this, click the **Full Range** button on the right.

After this, click on **Enter amounts**.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/06-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

Here, Swapr will calculate the token ratio based on the initial price and the selected price range. You must provide both tokens in the correct ratio.

Check if you have approved to send tokens to Swapr yet and click **Approve** if haven't.

<Frame><img src="/images/app/provide-liquidity/07-image.png" alt="" /></Frame>

After approving, click on **Add liquidity** to add liquidity to the pair.

Once the transaction is confirmed, you are now a liquidity provider (LP) on Swapr. You can review your position on [https://v3.swapr.eth.limo/#/pool](https://v3.swapr.eth.limo/#/pool).
